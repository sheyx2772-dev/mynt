# FLEX karta dizaynlari — AI promptlar

Har bir prompt **bitta kartaning old yuzasini** chiqaradi. Foydalanuvchi
kabinetdan tanlaydi, biz shu dizaynda bosib chiqaramiz.

## Har safar oldiga qo'yiladigan asos

Quyidagi matnni **har bir promptning boshiga** qo'ying, keyin tanlagan
uslubni qo'shing:

```text
Flat front-face artwork for an NFC business card, exact aspect ratio 1.586:1
(standard bank card, 85.6 x 54 mm), viewed straight on, no perspective, no
mockup, no hand, no background scene — the card fills the entire frame.

Layout rules that must be respected:
- Leave the lower-left quarter clear and calm: a handle code like "AAA000" and
  the line "flex.com.uz/AAA000" are printed there afterwards, so no busy detail
  or bright colour in that area.
- Leave the top-right corner clear for a small contactless NFC symbol.
- No text of any kind in the artwork itself. No logos, no letters, no numbers.
- Keep detail bold enough to survive printing at 85 mm wide: no hairlines
  thinner than 0.3 mm, no tiny texture.
- Print-safe colour: avoid pure neon that cannot be reproduced in CMYK.

Style:
```

## 1-guruh — Kasb bo'yicha

### 1.1 Shifokor / tibbiyot
> Style: calm clinical white and soft cool grey, one continuous thin line
> crossing the card like a heartbeat trace, flattening to a straight line at the
> edges. A single desaturated teal accent. Restrained, trustworthy, hospital-clean.

### 1.2 Yurist / advokat
> Style: deep navy with a subtle blind-embossed classical column motif at the
> right edge, one thin brushed-gold rule running horizontally. Sober, formal,
> old-institution weight. No ornament in the lower left.

### 1.3 IT / dasturchi
> Style: near-black with a faint circuit-trace pattern etched at low contrast,
> lines meeting at right angles, one lime-green trace lit brighter than the rest
> and running toward the top-right corner. Technical, quiet, not gamer-loud.

### 1.4 Quruvchi / muhandis
> Style: dark graphite with a faint blueprint grid, one bold diagonal safety-orange
> band across the upper third, and a subtle hatched texture like technical drawing
> shading. Industrial, sturdy.

### 1.5 O'qituvchi / ustoz
> Style: warm deep green like an old chalkboard, faint chalk-dust texture, one
> hand-drawn underline stroke in soft white. Calm, human, unpretentious.

### 1.6 Fotograf / ijodkor
> Style: matte black with a large soft circular lens-flare bloom bleeding from the
> top-right corner, faint concentric aperture blades. Cinematic, moody, minimal.

### 1.7 Oshpaz / restoran
> Style: deep charcoal with a warm copper gradient sweeping from the right edge,
> faint texture of hammered metal. Rich, appetising, premium kitchen.

### 1.8 Go'zallik / beauty
> Style: soft blush-nude base with a pearlescent sheen, one thin rose-gold arc
> curving through the upper half. Delicate, expensive, feminine without cliche.

### 1.9 Haydovchi / logistika
> Style: asphalt-dark with a faint road-surface texture, two parallel light streaks
> like long-exposure headlights sweeping from the left. Motion, night, distance.

## 2-guruh — Lavozim va jiddiylik darajasi

### 2.1 Rahbar / direktor
> Style: pure matte black, completely empty except for a single hairline of brushed
> silver running the full width at the vertical centre. Maximum restraint — the
> confidence of showing almost nothing.

### 2.2 Katta mutaxassis
> Style: dark slate with a very subtle vertical brushed-metal grain, one narrow
> lime band along the left edge only. Quiet seniority.

### 2.3 Yosh mutaxassis
> Style: deep charcoal base with one large soft lime-to-teal gradient blob glowing
> from the bottom-right, blurred like a light source behind frosted glass. Modern,
> energetic, still professional.

### 2.4 Yorqin / ochiq xarakter
> Style: bold flat lime-green field with a black geometric wedge cutting in from the
> right edge. High contrast, confident, impossible to miss on a table.

## 3-guruh — Milliy va madaniy

Bu yerda mualliflik masalasi yo'q — bular umumxalq merosi, va aynan shu
guruh sizni xalqaro raqobatchilardan ajratib turadi.

### 3.1 Suzani
> Style: deep indigo base with a large traditional Uzbek suzani floral medallion
> rendered as fine tone-on-tone embroidery texture, only slightly lighter than the
> base so it reads as texture, not decoration. One warm gold accent thread.

### 3.2 Ikat / abrbandi
> Style: the blurred-edge warp pattern of Uzbek ikat silk, vertical bands of deep
> teal, cream and rust with the characteristic feathered colour bleed. Rich, woven,
> unmistakably Central Asian.

### 3.3 Panjara / geometrik
> Style: black base with an intricate Islamic geometric lattice — interlaced
> eight-point stars — etched at low contrast in dark bronze, denser toward the
> right edge and fading toward the lower left.

### 3.4 Registon
> Style: deep lapis blue with the silhouette of tiled dome and iwan arch geometry
> abstracted into flat shapes, small gold tile fragments catching light. Monumental,
> historic, proud.

### 3.5 Cho'l va tog'
> Style: layered flat silhouettes of Uzbek mountain ridges in graduated warm sand
> and dusk-violet tones, a thin lime horizon line where earth meets sky.

### 4.2 Futbol

### 4.3 Gaming
> Style: near-black with a subtle hexagonal mesh, two thin light trails in magenta

### 4.4 Anime uslubi
> Style: flat two-tone poster illustration in the manner of Japanese animation cels

### 4.5 Ilmiy fantastika

> the bottom edge catching a thin lime atmospheric rim light. Vast, quiet.

### 4.6 Musiqa
> Style: dark charcoal with a horizontal audio waveform rendered as fine vertical
> bars, tallest at the centre and decaying toward both edges, in graduated lime.

### 4.7 Tabiat
> Style: deep forest green with large botanical leaf silhouettes overlapping at the
> right edge in tone-on-tone shades, faint organic paper texture.

   `1-3-it.jpg`, `3-1-suzani.jpg` va hokazo

tanlaymiz.
